🌿 The basics

Be respectful, keep conversations welcoming and give everyone room to enjoy the server.

Staff may step in when rules are ignored. Repeated issues can lead to moderation action.

Be kind and respectful

No harassment, hate, bullying or deliberately starting fights.

Keep content appropriate

Use the right channels and follow Discord's rules. Do not post inappropriate or unsafe content.

Respect privacy

No spam, unwanted DMs, sharing private information or pressuring members.

Listen to the staff team

Follow moderator instructions and use the proper channels if you need help.
